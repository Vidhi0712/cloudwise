## 1. Platform Architecture & Concept

### The Problem
Organizations running cloud infrastructure on AWS face severe challenges with **cloud bill shock**, **oversized virtual machines**, and **idle database/storage instances**. Native cloud consoles show confusing raw numbers without explaining:
- *Why* a bill went up.
- *Which* resources are being wasted.
- *What specific action* an engineer should take.

### The CloudWise Solution
CloudWise bridges raw infrastructure metrics and human decision-making using:
1. **Unsupervised ML Anomaly Detection** (Scikit-Learn Isolation Forest).
2. **Deterministic FinOps Utilization Rules** (CloudWatch metric baselines).
3. **Explainable AI/Recommendation Layer** that answers:
   - **WHAT is wrong?**
   - **WHY was it flagged?**
   - **WHAT should the user do?**
   - **HOW MUCH money will they save?** (in ₹ Indian Rupees)
   - **WHAT is the risk and confidence?**

## 5. Cost Anomalies (`/anomalies`) — AI Detection

### How the AI Anomaly Detection Works
- **Algorithm:** Scikit-Learn's **Isolation Forest** (`contamination=0.08`, `n_estimators=40`).
- **Input Features:** `[daily_cost, 7-day rolling_mean]`.
- **Methodology:** The algorithm isolates data points that deviate from the normal cluster density. When a daily cost record is flagged with prediction `-1`, CloudWise computes the percentage deviation relative to the historical baseline mean:
  $$\text{Deviation (\%)} = \frac{\text{Actual Cost} - \text{Baseline Mean}}{\text{Baseline Mean}} \times 100$$
- **Severity Tiers:**
  - **Critical (Red badge):** Deviation $> +50\%$
  - **High (Orange badge):** Deviation $> +30\%$
  - **Medium (Yellow badge):** Deviation $> +20\%$
  - **Low (Blue badge):** Deviation $< 20\%$

### What You See on the Page
- **Summary Badges:** Real-time counter of Critical and High alerts.
- **Filter Bar:** Filter anomalies by severity (`Critical`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`) or AWS Service.
- **Anomaly Cards:** Each card displays:
  - Resource Name & ID
  - Date of occurrence
  - Actual Cost (e.g. `₹598`) vs. Historical Average (e.g. `₹348`)
  - Calculated Deviation (e.g. `+71.7%`)
  - **Generated Explanation:**  
    *"Daily cost of ₹598 is 72% higher than the historical average of ₹348. This deviation is outside the normal spending pattern for this resource."*

## 8. Viva / Professor Demonstration Cheat Sheet

If asked by an evaluator or professor, here is how to explain the project in 60 seconds:

> *"CloudWise is an AI-assisted Cloud FinOps platform. The primary problem we solve is that cloud bills are opaque and cloud dashboards only tell you what you spent, not **why** you spent it or **how to fix it**.*
> 
> *Our novelty is the **Explainable Recommendation Layer**. We combine:*
> 1. *Machine Learning (**Isolation Forest**) to flag unexpected cost spikes without manual thresholds.*
> 2. *Resource telemetry heuristics to find idle or oversized EC2, RDS, S3, and Lambda instances.*
> 3. *A deterministic explanation engine that gives every recommendation a concrete monthly saving in ₹, a risk assessment, a confidence percentage, and an explicit plain-English reason based on observed telemetry.*
> 
> *For example, on our dashboard, CloudWise identified **₹15,658/month of potential savings** across 8 opportunities, including an underutilized EC2 instance (`production-api-03`) running at only 5.7% CPU that can be safely downsized to save ₹3,240/month with 92% confidence."*
